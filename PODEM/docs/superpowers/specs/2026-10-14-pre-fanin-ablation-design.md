# Pre-fanin GAT ablation design

Use the GAT-GRU policy at commit `7d92ab8`: its two-output Actor scores the objective gate embedding and objective value, without a candidate fanin embedding. Keep this checkout separate from the current fanin scorer.

Prepare one GAT training manifest from the dataset, or accept an existing compatible manifest. Train three independent ablation runs from that manifest with the same seed, reward, backtrack limit, and two-round training protocol, concurrently on three distinct GPUs. Do not consume or train a `full` run. `no_lock` disables the backtrace path lock in training and validation. `no_object_value` removes the objective value from Actor and Critic inputs while PODEM still uses it for logic. `no_cache` has its own checkpoint and actor, and disables native Actor logits caching during validation. Training uses the Python callback path, so the cache switch does not alter its training computation.

Validation runs only the three independently trained ablations on one ordered validation fault catalog, selecting each group's best round separately. It records per-fault outcomes and compares per-circuit and total detected, redundant, aborted, coverage, backtracks, backtrace steps, and ATPG time. The runner rejects mismatched manifest, seed, checkpoint variant, path-lock state, or shared model directories. Training and validation are separate explicit commands and use isolated artifact directories. No experiment is launched automatically by implementation or tests.
